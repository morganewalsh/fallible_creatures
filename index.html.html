About the lab

Fallible Creatures is an R&D lab for wonder.

Manifesto

We pair people who build models with people who dream: poets, climbers, birders, historians, anyone who looks at the world and asks a strange question. Together we build odd, small, wonderful things that nobody needed until they existed.

We are mortal, fallible creatures. Every time we learn something true about the world, we accept in the same moment that it might be untrue, or that it has opened another unknown worth chasing. We would rather guess boldly and be wrong in public than wait to be told what to think.

So we infer wildly. We look everywhere for clues: in the flight of birds before a storm, in the shape of a shell, in the knot at the bottom of a drawer. Not every partnership has to become another app. We can get weirder.
